No group left behind

No gap worse than baseline

Every same-acuity gap must stay at or below its baseline and trend down. A gap widening 2 months in a row pauses the tool.

Reach parity

Patient-link reach: highest group minus lowest group ≤ 10 points, by payer group.

Patients without phones

Covered by nurse rounding: a waiting-room nurse logs the same symptom update.

Insurance is never a model input

Used only to measure gaps, shown in the fairness panel in the demo.

Sources: ROADMAP.md §2.2, docs/BUDGET_ROI.md §3.3, docs/RISK_SECURITY.md §4.3

Equity means no group is left behind, so we set two rules. No gap may be worse than its baseline, and the tool must reach every payer group within ten points of each other. Patients without phones are reached by nurse rounding, and insurance is used only to measure gaps, never to score anyone.
