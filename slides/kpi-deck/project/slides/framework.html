Five families of KPIs

Operations

Are patients seen faster? Door-to-provider time and walk-outs (LWBS).

Equity

Are the gaps between payer groups closing, at the same acuity level?

Safety

Is anyone harmed or sent away too soon? Bounce-backs, 72-hour returns, incidents.

Adoption

Do nurses use and trust it? Override rate and nurse ratings.

Patient reach

Does every group get the updates? Patient-link reach by payer group.

Averages can improve while the patients who were already behind stay behind.

Sources: ROADMAP.md §2.1, docs/BUDGET_ROI.md §3.3

We group our measures into five families. Operations tells us if we are faster, and equity tells us if the speed-up reaches everyone. The key message is that an average can improve while the patients who were already behind stay behind, which is why equity sits at the center.
