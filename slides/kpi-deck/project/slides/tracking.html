How we track it

Weekly

Dashboard by shift and group. Override review at huddles.

Monthly

Committee: overrides, bounce-back, 72-hr returns, equity metrics.

Quarterly

Fairness audit and clinical committee oversight, reported to the board.

Green: on target

Continue and publish results openly.

Amber: off track

Gap not shrinking 2 months: audit scores and overrides by group; add languages and nurse rounding.

Red: pause

Safety event, bounce-back ≥ 5% for 2 weeks, gap widening 2 months, override rate outside 10–30% for a month, security incident.

Phase-2 gate: equity criterion: same-acuity gaps for uninsured and Medicaid patients no wider than baseline, and trending down.

Sources: ROADMAP.md §2.1–2.2, docs/RISK_SECURITY.md §4.3

We review weekly, monthly and quarterly. Green means on target, amber triggers the fix actions in the roadmap, and red means the medical director can pause the tool. To move to Phase 2, the equity gaps must be no wider than baseline and trending down.
