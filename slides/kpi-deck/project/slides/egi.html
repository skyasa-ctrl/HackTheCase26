Equity Gap Index (EGI)

EGI = average of (current gap ÷ baseline gap) × 100, across the 4 gaps

100 = today. Below 100 = gaps shrinking. Above 100 = widening. Two months running pauses the tool.

The Equity Gap Index rolls the four gaps into one number, where one hundred means today. If every target is met, the index falls to about thirty-four by Month 18. The line shows our target path, not results, and the Month-6 point is a planning value we will confirm with the committee.
